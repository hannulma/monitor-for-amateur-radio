PRIVACY

Monitor for Amateur Radio collects no data about its users for the maintainer: no telemetry, analytics or advertising. Settings, logs and stored data stay on this computer.

The program contacts external services only while the corresponding feature is in use:
- BrandMeister (core function: Last Heard feed and lookups; Hoseline while listening)
- APRS-IS, when APRS messaging or feeds to your radio are enabled
- GitHub: update check once a day (can be switched off in Settings)
- open data services (e.g. FMI, MeteoAlarm, NOAA, Digitraffic, CelesTrak), only those switched on in Services
- OpenStreetMap tiles while viewing the APRS coverage map
- feeds and calendars you add yourself, and a check-in sheet if you connect one
- feedback to the maintainer only when you send it yourself (secrets are removed)

Every feature can be switched off in the program's settings. Full privacy policy:
https://github.com/hannulma/monitor-for-amateur-radio/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
